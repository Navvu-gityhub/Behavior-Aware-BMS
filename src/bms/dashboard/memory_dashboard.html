Capacity health is the shipped voltage-window estimator, unchanged across datasets: 1.7% median error on CALCE LCO cells, 3.0% on Oxford NMC/LCO pouch cells at 40 °C, 2.3–2.8% on LG M50T 21700 cells at 32–46 °C. It is weak on cold cells (NASA, 4 °C: 12–14%), and confidence does not always flag that. The reference is the first discharges this service saw, not necessarily the cell as new, so confidence is at most MEDIUM. Early remaining life is refused: two tested attempts failed.
