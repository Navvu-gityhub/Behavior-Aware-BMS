## Reading the result

The hypothesis under test (arm C, siblings) failed criteria 1 and 2.
- **Unsafe bound.** Its lower bound held for only 41% of the deciding cells,
  with the bound above the true life on the median cell. Such a bound would
  overstate remaining life.
- **Likely cause.** The slope learned on full-range cycling (Expt 4+5,
  b = -1.65) does not carry to the partial state-of-charge windows of
  Expt 1-3.

The comparison arm B (the cell's own early fade rate, extrapolated, nothing
learned) erred 7.7% on the deciding cells, against 20.0% in development. It was
pre-specified as a baseline, with no acceptance criteria and no lower bound.
Its deciding-set accuracy is therefore a lead to confirm on fresh cells, not a
validated claim. BEACON keeps refusing early RUL.
