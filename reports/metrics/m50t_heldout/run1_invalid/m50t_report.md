## STATUS: INVALID - the measurement failed, not the correction

Found after the run by inspecting raw samples at discharge onset:

1. 556 of 570 discharges of cell D (and similarly the others) begin straight
   from the constant-voltage charge tail (~50 mA at 4.20 V) with no rest.
   The shipped step detector requires |I| <= 0.02 A before the load, so it
   found a step in only ~14 discharges per cell.
2. Most of those steps are logger artefacts: 0.3-0.4 milliohm, where the raw
   data shows a clean 27.7 milliohm step (4.1811 -> 4.0424 V at 5 A). The
   trailing-median resistance was dominated by them, so the ohmic correction
   collapsed to ~0 for most of life: the 25/40 C errors (~10%) follow.
3. The anchored arm needs a resistance level over its reference discharges;
   none existed, so it produced no readings at all.

Scored as it ran, the verdict line above says NOT MET; it is not a test of
the correction. A rerun after the step detector is repaired is reported
separately and labelled as such.
