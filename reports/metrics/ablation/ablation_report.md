Reading it:

- Neither half transfers alone. XGBoost on cycle number only, and on behaviour features only, both score below zero under LOCO; only the combination reaches the published point estimate, and its interval still overlaps the others.
- The linear age baseline (`age_linear`) beats XGBoost on cycle number alone under LOCO: trees cannot extrapolate cycle count to an unseen cohort, a straight line can.
- With each cell's capacity shuffled in time, every LOCO interval lies at or below zero. The harness does not produce skill where there is none. Residual LOBO skill for XGBoost on the shuffled frame is per-cell level memorisation: a cell's mean capacity survives the shuffle.
