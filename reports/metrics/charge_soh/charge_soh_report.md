## Status and reading

**Development evidence, not a validated result.** The "deciding" sets
(M50T, Oxford) had already had their DISCHARGE results examined, so even a
pass here would have been weaker proof than a fresh test. The fresh test is
the rig's own charges (docs/bench_test_guide.md, stage 3), with criteria to
be written before they are recorded.

- **Oxford, 0.8%.** The 1C characterisation charges, a steady and regular
  charge, read within 0.8% on all 8 cells, better than the same cells'
  discharge window (3.0%).
- **M50T, 4.3%.** The 0.3C charges read on 8 of 8 cells, but missed the 3%
  criterion.
- **NASA, 8 of 31 cells.** Its 1.5 A charges reach the 4.2 V hold early. With
  the I*R shift, the window moves into the hold and is refused.

Health-while-charging is not shipped.
