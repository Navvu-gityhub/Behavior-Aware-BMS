# Field SOH: voltage, current and time only

Generated by `scripts/run_field_soh_study.py`. Each CALCE cell's raw cycler log is reduced to what a vehicle BMS has - voltage, current, time - and the pipeline segments the discharges, builds the curves, estimates its own resistance from the rest-to-load step and measures SOH from the voltage window. Nothing the cycler computed reaches the estimate.

Error is mean absolute error in SOH (0.01 = one percentage point), per cell, against cycler capacity over the median of the cell's first five cycles - the same reference the estimator uses. The median is across cells; the 95% interval resamples cells. `median_cell_mae_lifetime_ref` is the same estimate scored against a lifetime-percentile reference, for comparison with `calce_voltage_window/`.
